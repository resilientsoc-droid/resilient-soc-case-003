Core Architecture & Topology\nThe environment is structured to separate detection, case management, enrichment, and orchestration across integrated nodes:\n\n* **Host / OS:** Kali Linux (Host environment)\n* **TheHive (v5.7.5 / v5.2):** Case & Incident Management (`http://192.168.1.11:9000`)\n* **Cortex (v4.1.0 / v3.1.7):** Automated IOC Enrichment (`http://192.168.1.11:9001/cortex`)\n* **Shuffle SOAR:** Playbook Automation & Orchestration (`http://192.168.1.11:3001`)\n* **Splunk:** Detection Engineering & Alert Trigger Source\n* **Custom Flask Webhook Integration Layer (`soar_webhook.py`):** Acts as the bridge processing `approve`/`reject` actions and programmatically pushing structured audit logs to TheHive API (`POST /api/v1/case/{case_id}/log`).\n\n---\n\n## 3.
